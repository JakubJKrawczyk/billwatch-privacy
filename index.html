BillWatch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

BillWatch is a simple bill reminder app for Android published by NevoGear (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: your data stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

Data the app stores

The bills you enter (name, optional amount, number of payments, interval and which bills are marked as paid) are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded, synced or shared with anyone, including us.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include BillWatch data in your personal device backup and restore it on a new phone. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

Data the app collects or shares

BillWatch does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to remind you about unpaid bills. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
  • Run at startup – so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual bills in the app at any time. Uninstalling BillWatch or clearing its storage in Android settings removes all of its data from your device.

Children

BillWatch is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from them.
